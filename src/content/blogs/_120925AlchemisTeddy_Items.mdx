Developed as part of the asset range of AlchemisTeddy, this set of bottles combines hand-painted and shader-driven approaches. 
Five bottles have subtle shading variations, each featuring a specific hand-painted detail. In addition, two bottles use a custom shader.

The shader was designed with flexibility in mind: it simulates a nebulous liquid that can be adjusted for fill level, 
allowing the same model to represent multiple potion types. The artist provided custom noise textures, which are integrated into the shader to 
ensure visual differentiation across the bottles.
